## Eligibility gate

`DisplayGate.evaluate()` accepts a device only when all checks pass:

1. manufacturer/brand is **Samsung** or **Xiaomi/Redmi/POCO**;
2. the vendor route exists:
   - Samsung: `Settings.Secure.refresh_rate_mode`;
   - Xiaomi: `Settings.Secure.miui_refresh_rate` or `user_refresh_rate`;
3. the display exposes **at least two distinct refresh rates**.

**LTPO is not required.** A 120/90/60/30 panel is accepted. If any check fails,
OneHZ shows “Display not supported” with the reason and does not write anything.

Check the device locally:

```powershell
adb shell getprop ro.product.manufacturer
adb shell getprop ro.product.model
adb shell getprop ro.product.brand
adb shell getprop ro.build.version.release
adb shell getprop ro.build.version.oneui
adb shell getprop ro.mi.os.version.name
adb shell dumpsys display | Select-String "mSupportedRefreshRates"
adb shell settings get secure refresh_rate_mode
adb shell settings get secure miui_refresh_rate
adb shell settings get secure user_refresh_rate
```

## Supported devices

- Samsung phones with at least two refresh rates.
- Xiaomi/Redmi/POCO phones on HyperOS/MIUI with at least two refresh rates.
- Tested only on the S25+ and the POCO X7 Pro above.

## Panel limitations

### 30 Hz brightness on HyperOS

On the tested POCO X7 Pro (`2412DPC0AG`), 30 Hz is visibly darker than 60/120 Hz at the
same system brightness. The difference is generated by the panel/vendor PQ when the
display mode changes, not by Android:

- `screen_brightness` and `debug.tracing.screen_brightness` are identical at 30 and 120 Hz;
- the framebuffer is identical (same content, same pixels);
- the reported backlight is identical;
- the vendor dimming mode (`DisplayFeatureManager` `setScreenEffect`) and
  `cmd display set-brightness` do not change it;
- Shizuku runs as `shell` (UID 2000, no root), and the vendor sysfs/property routes that
  could change the panel gamma are denied by SELinux.

OneHZ therefore cannot equalize the brightness. Keeping 30 Hz is supported, and the
profile editor shows a warning on Xiaomi devices when a step uses 30 Hz. At maximum
brightness there is no headroom to compensate upward, and dimming 60/120 Hz to match
30 Hz would only make the whole screen darker.

## Known limitations

- **Samsung, Xiaomi, Redmi and POCO only.** Other manufacturers are rejected by the
  compatibility gate.
- **Multi-rate display required.** The gate requires at least two refresh rates; LTPO
  is not required.
- **Two-rate panels.** Economy, Balanced and Performance use the same two bands and
  differ only by the hold at the maximum. The time at the minimum is the resting time
  until the next touch.
- **AOD minimum.** The AOD uses the lowest rate exposed by the panel. On panels that
  reject very low rates in AOD, the engine falls back to 30 Hz and logs the failure.
- **Two devices tested.** Samsung S25+ `SM-S936B` (Android 17 / One UI 9.0) and
  POCO X7 Pro `2412DPC0AG` (Android 16 / HyperOS 3.0).
- Other Android/One UI/HyperOS versions and WQHD+ are untested.
- Shizuku must be running. After a reboot, it may need to be restarted through wireless
  debugging/ADB.
- Display detection depends on firmware settings and `dumpsys` output; a future
  firmware update may change them. HyperOS uses `miui_refresh_rate` (or
  `user_refresh_rate` on HyperOS 1).
- AOD brightness is **Samsung-only** (`debug_aod_brightness`); it is hidden on Xiaomi.
- **Sleep (forced Doze) is vendor-dependent.** Some ROMs ignore or quickly revert the
  forced idle; the card reports “The ROM did not enter Doze” instead of pretending it
  worked.
- **HyperOS light idle:** on the tested POCO X7 Pro, deep idle is forced
  (`mForceIdle=true`), while light idle is already the normal screen-off state
  (`mState=IDLE`, `mLightState=OVERRIDE`) and does not set `mForceIdle`.
- **HyperOS screen-off delivery:** on the same device, the `SCREEN_OFF` broadcast was
  occasionally delivered about 10 seconds after the key event while the app was in the
  background (`QUICK_DOZE_DELAY`); the foreground service still received it and the force
  was applied afterwards.
- **Sleep measurements include the AOD.** On devices with Always On Display, the
  screen-off window also contains AOD draw, so the percent per hour is not a pure
  deep-idle figure.
- Video detection depends on MediaSession, Codec2, and AudioService behavior; the rules
  and a forced app list can be tuned in the app, but the underlying signals must exist.
- PiP video is not detected yet.
- If Android kills the app while a non-default rate is applied (for example, due to
  aggressive battery management), the last rate may remain until OneHZ starts again.
  Grant the optional battery exemption or reopen the app to restore the system default.
- Do not run another refresh-rate controller at the same time.
- No warranty. This is a personal project.

## Not supported / untested

- other manufacturers (blocked by the gate);
- devices with a single refresh rate (blocked);
- Samsung without `refresh_rate_mode` (blocked);
- Xiaomi without `miui_refresh_rate`/`user_refresh_rate` (blocked);
- AOD brightness on Xiaomi (Samsung-only; hidden);
- foldables and cover screens (untested);
- tablets (untested);
- WQHD+ mode on the tested Samsung (untested);
- Android 12–15 (minSdk allows them, but only Android 16 and 17 were tested);
- HyperOS 1/2 (the route fallback exists, but only HyperOS 3 was tested);
- other Shizuku versions.

## Firmware dependencies

Samsung:

- `refresh_rate_mode`;
- `peak_refresh_rate`, `min_refresh_rate`;
- `debug_aod_brightness`;
- Samsung battery-saver rate keys (`pms_*`/`psm_*`).

Xiaomi/HyperOS:

- `miui_refresh_rate` (HyperOS 2/3);
- `user_refresh_rate` (HyperOS 1 fallback).

A future firmware update may rename, restrict, or remove these settings. In that case,
OneHZ fails closed and shows the detected state. Please report the firmware build and
the diagnostic report.

## Shizuku

- Tested with the **thedjchi fork `v13.7.0-thedjchi`** on both the S25+ and the POCO X7
  Pro: <https://github.com/thedjchi/Shizuku/tree/v13.7.0-thedjchi>
- The official [Shizuku](https://github.com/RikkaApps/Shizuku) should work because the
  app uses the standard API, but it was not tested.
- After a reboot, Shizuku may need to be started again through wireless debugging or
  ADB. OneHZ reconnects when Shizuku becomes available.

## Reporting compatibility

Include:

- model, product, Android, One UI/HyperOS, build, security patch;
- panel rates from `dumpsys display`;
- route values (`refresh_rate_mode`, `miui_refresh_rate`, `user_refresh_rate`);
- Shizuku version;
- whether the gate shows OK;
- what works and what does not.

Do not post personal data.
