# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a respectful experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good faith and focus on the technical problem.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully.
- Respect privacy: do not publish other people's logs, package names, or personal data.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Spam, advertising, or repeated off-topic comments.
- Encouraging piracy, license violations, or distribution of closed-source code/assets.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, remove, or reject comments, issues, and pull requests that
violate this code. Repeated or serious violations may result in a block from the
repository.

## Reporting

Report problems privately through the repository's security advisory or by contacting
the maintainer through GitHub. Do not open a public issue with personal data.
